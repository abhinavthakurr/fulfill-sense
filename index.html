The engine

Not a dashboard.
A statistical opinion.

Rule-based fraud checks fail because fraud is not linear. The FulfillSense core is an ensemble of one hundred decision trees that learns the interactions a human would never write down.

01

Random Forest ensemble

One hundred trees vote on every order, surfacing the non-linear correlations in shopper behaviour that brittle if-then rules structurally cannot see.

02

Dynamic feature weighting

A ₹6,000 cart is not risky on its own. It becomes risky at 3am, on COD, from a guest checkout. The model weighs the combination, not the variable.

03

Explainable by default

Every score ships with the reason behind it, computed with SHAP values, so a warehouse manager can defend the call instead of trusting a black box.

Architecture

Three steps from CSV
to quarantine

Designed to sit quietly between your storefront export and your warehouse management system.

1

Ingestion

Drop the daily unfulfilled-orders export from Shopify or WooCommerce. No integration work, no webhooks to maintain.

2

Evaluation

The serialized model deserializes and scores the batch, returning a calibrated RTO probability for every single line.

3

Quarantine

Anything above your threshold is isolated for an OTP confirmation call instead of being handed to a courier.

In practice

Catch the losses.
Never the loyalists.

Multi-variable anomaly detection

Fraud never shows up in one column. The model reads guest checkout status, cart sizing, payment mode and order timing as a single matrix, intercepting high-probability returns before dispatch.

Protecting genuine buyers

Blunt rules block every high-value order. The forest learns that a registered customer who prepays is among your safest revenue, and lets your best buyers through untouched.
